# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shoppers in Iraq, especially people browsing the store on a phone, who want to inspect a product clearly, compare its available colors and stock, and place an order with local delivery.

## Product Purpose

EUIQ helps shoppers discover products, review accurate product details and media, and buy with clear Iraqi delivery and payment information.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trustworthy, and approachable. Store copy uses Baghdadi Iraqi Arabic where appropriate.

## Anti-references

- Recolored or rebranded interfaces that discard EUIQ's existing identity.
- Default, illustrative, or unrelated imagery presented as real product media.
- Product images cropped or stretched in ways that hide the product.
- Cluttered product pages that make the media, price, stock, or purchase action hard to find.

## Design Principles

- Show the actual product clearly before asking shoppers to buy.
- Keep product images and video easy to browse on phones and desktop screens.
- Keep availability, price, options, and purchase actions easy to scan.
- Use familiar Baghdadi wording and clear local delivery and payment details.
- Preserve the established EUIQ visual identity across improvements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast and clear labels, make media controls and image selection keyboard accessible, keep touch targets usable on phones, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
